# House Themes for Boundary Planet Testing

Use these descriptions to build "House A vs House B" questions.
Frame questions using the PLANET-SPECIFIC themes, not generic house meanings.
For example: "Does your discipline (Saturn) manifest through social networks (11th) or through isolation and behind-the-scenes work (12th)?"

---

## House 1: Self, Body, Identity

**Career/Income**: Self-made, personal brand matters, career tied to physical presence or identity
**Relationships**: Independent in partnerships, self-focused, partner must respect autonomy
**Health**: Constitutional health, vitality, prone to head/face issues
**Personality**: Strong sense of self, visible to others, personality defines life path
**Everyday Patterns**: Life revolves around personal expression, self-improvement, physical activity

---

## House 2: Wealth, Speech, Family

**Career/Income**: Income through speech, knowledge, or family business; accumulation of resources; banking/finance
**Relationships**: Family dynamics dominate social life, values-based connections, oral tradition
**Health**: Mouth, teeth, throat, right eye; dietary habits affect health significantly
**Personality**: Values-driven, concerned with security, good or distinctive voice/speech patterns
**Everyday Patterns**: Saving vs spending tension, strong food preferences, family obligations structure daily life

---

## House 3: Courage, Siblings, Communication

**Career/Income**: Income through writing, media, short travel, sales, or communication
**Relationships**: Siblings are significant, neighborhood connections matter, casual social network
**Health**: Hands, arms, shoulders, ears; nervous system, respiratory
**Personality**: Courageous, initiative-taking, self-taught skills, communicative
**Everyday Patterns**: Short trips frequent, busy with multiple small tasks, hands-on work, learning through doing

---

## House 4: Home, Mother, Inner Peace

**Career/Income**: Real estate, vehicles, agriculture, work from home, domestic industries
**Relationships**: Mother is a dominant figure, emotional security through home, need for rootedness
**Health**: Chest, heart, lungs; emotional health tied to home stability
**Personality**: Needs a secure base, nostalgic, patriotic, emotionally grounded (or seeking grounding)
**Everyday Patterns**: Home-centric life, real estate matters, vehicle importance, attachment to birthplace

---

## House 5: Creativity, Intelligence, Children

**Career/Income**: Creative professions, speculation/investment, entertainment, education, coaching
**Relationships**: Romantic affairs (before marriage), relationship with children, creative partnerships
**Health**: Stomach, upper abdomen; pregnancy-related (for applicable persons)
**Personality**: Creative, playful, intellectually confident, speculative/risk-taking
**Everyday Patterns**: Hobbies and creative projects feature prominently, may gamble or speculate, attracted to performance

---

## House 6: Service, Enemies, Health Struggles

**Career/Income**: Service professions, healthcare, law, military, competitive fields; income through overcoming obstacles
**Relationships**: Conflict in relationships, competitor dynamics, enemies/rivals play a role
**Health**: Digestive system, intestines, immune system; chronic conditions requiring daily management
**Personality**: Problem-solver, service-oriented, finds satisfaction through overcoming obstacles, competitive
**Everyday Patterns**: Daily routines are crucial, health management is a constant theme, deals with conflicts regularly

---

## House 7: Partnership, Marriage, Others

**Career/Income**: Business partnerships, client-facing work, consulting, trade, negotiation-based careers
**Relationships**: Marriage and committed partnerships define life direction, strong need for a significant other
**Health**: Kidneys, lower back, reproductive system (external)
**Personality**: Defines self through relationships, diplomatic, negotiating, oriented toward the other
**Everyday Patterns**: Life organized around partner/partnerships, contracts and agreements matter, public-facing activities

---

## House 8: Transformation, Hidden Things, Crisis

**Career/Income**: Research, investigation, insurance, inheritance, occult, psychology, crisis management
**Relationships**: Intense, transformative relationships; sexuality as a significant life theme; shared resources
**Health**: Chronic or hidden diseases, reproductive system (internal), sudden health crises, longevity
**Personality**: Private, investigative, drawn to hidden knowledge, undergoes major life transformations
**Everyday Patterns**: Life punctuated by crises and rebirths, interest in mysteries/occult, dealing with others' resources

---

## House 9: Dharma, Fortune, Higher Knowledge

**Career/Income**: Teaching, law, religion, philosophy, publishing, long-distance travel, foreign connections
**Relationships**: Guru/mentor relationships important, father figure significant, philosophical companionship
**Health**: Hips, thighs; generally good health (fortunate house)
**Personality**: Philosophical, ethical, drawn to higher meaning, teacher archetype, righteous
**Everyday Patterns**: Education and learning continue throughout life, travel to distant places, religious/philosophical practice

---

## House 10: Career, Reputation, Public Status

**Career/Income**: Career IS the primary life channel; public reputation, government, authority, institutional position
**Relationships**: Professional relationships dominate, public persona affects personal life
**Health**: Knees, bones, joints; stress from career responsibilities
**Personality**: Ambitious, status-conscious, dutiful, identified by what they do professionally
**Everyday Patterns**: Work dominates daily life, public image carefully managed, career achievements are primary milestones

---

## House 11: Gains, Networks, Desires

**Career/Income**: Income through networks and associations, side projects, non-linear career paths; what you EARN vs what you DO
**Relationships**: Friendships are a primary life channel, elder siblings significant, social circles determine opportunities
**Health**: Left ear, left leg, ankles; generally good (upachaya house, improves over time)
**Personality**: Goal-oriented, future-focused, networking comes naturally, motivated by desire fulfillment
**Everyday Patterns**: Multiple income streams, social energy is fuel for everything else, large ambitions, opportunities come through people

---

## House 12: Loss, Isolation, Foreign, Spiritual

**Career/Income**: Work behind the scenes, in isolation, or in foreign lands; expenses match or exceed income; best work happens unseen
**Relationships**: Isolation in relationships, difficulty maintaining connections, spiritual companionship valued over social
**Health**: Feet, left eye, sleep disorders; hospitalization; health issues may be hidden or misdiagnosed
**Personality**: Introspective, spiritual, drawn to solitude, may appear detached; rich inner life that others don't see
**Everyday Patterns**: Lives in relative isolation (even if not by choice), foreign connections significant, sleep and dreams important, expenditure of energy on unseen things
