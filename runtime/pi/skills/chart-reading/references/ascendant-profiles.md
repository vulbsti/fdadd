# Ascendant (Rising Sign) Profiles for Rectification

Use these profiles to verify whether the calculated ascendant matches the native.
Ask about physical appearance AND personality — both should match for confirmation.

---

## Aries Rising (Mesha Lagna)

### Physical Appearance
- Medium height, athletic or wiry build, lean rather than heavy
- Angular face, prominent forehead or brow ridge
- Quick, energetic movements — walks fast, gestures sharply
- Tends to look younger than actual age
- Often has a scar or mark on the head/face

### Personality & First Impression
- Impatient, action-first, dislikes long deliberation
- Natural leader who resists being told what to do
- Quick temper but equally quick to forgive and move on
- Competitive in everything, even casual activities
- Pioneer energy — drawn to being first, starting things
- Direct speech, sometimes blunt to the point of tactlessness

### Nakshatra Refinements
#### Ashwini (0-13.33°)
- Healers and quick-fixers, restless energy, youthful throughout life
- Spontaneous, impulsive, may start many things and not finish
#### Bharani (13.33-26.67°)
- More intense and transformative, stronger creative/sexual energy
- Endurance beyond what the body looks capable of
#### Krittika (26.67-30°)
- Sharp critical mind, fiery speech, authoritative presence
- More disciplined than other Aries nakshatras, can be harsh in criticism

## Taurus Rising (Vrishabha Lagna)

### Physical Appearance
- Sturdy, solid build — broad shoulders, thick neck
- Round or square face, full lips, pleasant features
- Slow, deliberate movements — no unnecessary motion
- Good skin, often attractive or pleasant-looking
- Tends toward weight gain, especially later in life

### Personality & First Impression
- Calm, steady, grounded — the most stable person in the room
- Values comfort, beauty, and sensory pleasure (food, music, touch)
- Stubborn and resistant to change once settled
- Patient but volcanic when pushed past limits
- Practical and materialistic — values tangible results
- Loyal and dependable, strong dislike of instability

### Nakshatra Refinements
#### Krittika (0-10°)
- Sharper and more critical than typical Taurus, fiery undercurrent
- May have digestive issues, drawn to cooking/food preparation
#### Rohini (10-23.33°)
- Most attractive Taurus placement, creative, sensual, magnetic
- Love of luxury and beauty, artistic sensibility
#### Mrigashirsha (23.33-30°)
- Restless searching quality unlike typical Taurus stability
- Curious mind, always looking for something more, intellectual interests

## Gemini Rising (Mithuna Lagna)

### Physical Appearance
- Lean, medium height, youthful appearance regardless of age
- Expressive face and hands — talks with entire body
- Quick, darting eyes, animated facial expressions
- Nervous energy visible in body — fidgeting, restless
- Often thin or wiry build, rarely heavy

### Personality & First Impression
- Quick mind, fast speech, juggles multiple interests simultaneously
- Curious about everything, easily bored, needs constant stimulation
- Socially adaptable — can talk to anyone about anything
- Dualistic — can seem inconsistent or contradictory
- Information gatherer, loves learning but may lack depth
- Witty, humorous, uses language skillfully

### Nakshatra Refinements
#### Mrigashirsha (0-6.67°)
- Searching, never fully satisfied, always looking for the next thing
- Gentle and creative, more sensitive than other Gemini placements
#### Ardra (6.67-20°)
- More intense and emotionally stormy, transformation through crisis
- Sharp intellect, can be cutting in speech, passionate about ideas
#### Punarvasu (20-30°)
- More optimistic and nurturing, return to roots after exploration
- Teaching ability, philosophical bent, broader perspective

## Cancer Rising (Karka Lagna)

### Physical Appearance
- Round face, soft features, nurturing appearance
- Medium build, tendency toward water retention and roundness
- Expressive eyes that show emotion easily
- Gentle demeanor, protective body language
- May have a prominent chest or midsection

### Personality & First Impression
- Emotionally sensitive, mood shifts visible externally
- Nurturing and protective of family and close circle
- Strong attachment to home, mother, and roots
- Indirect communication — rarely says things directly
- Excellent memory, especially for emotional events
- Can be clingy or possessive when insecure

### Nakshatra Refinements
#### Punarvasu (0-3.33°)
- More philosophical and optimistic, less emotionally reactive
- Ability to bounce back from setbacks
#### Pushya (3.33-16.67°)
- Most nurturing Cancer placement, devoted to service and care
- Conservative, traditional values, strong sense of duty
#### Ashlesha (16.67-30°)
- More intense and psychologically penetrating, manipulative when cornered
- Magnetic quality, deep understanding of human psychology

## Leo Rising (Simha Lagna)

### Physical Appearance
- Broad frame, commanding presence, takes up space naturally
- Mane-like hair or distinctive head/hair feature
- Proud posture — walks with chest out, head high
- Warm, radiant smile, generous facial expressions
- Often tall or at least appears tall due to bearing

### Personality & First Impression
- Natural authority — people look to them for leadership without being asked
- Generous, warm, loves giving and being appreciated for it
- Strong ego — needs recognition and respect
- Creative and dramatic, enjoys being center of attention
- Loyal and fiercely protective of those they claim as "theirs"
- Can be arrogant, domineering, or unable to accept criticism

### Nakshatra Refinements
#### Magha (0-13.33°)
- Regal and traditional, strong connection to ancestry and lineage
- Natural authority, drawn to power and prestige
#### Purva Phalguni (13.33-26.67°)
- More pleasure-seeking and creative, love of arts and entertainment
- Charismatic, romantic, enjoys luxury and celebration
#### Uttara Phalguni (26.67-30°)
- More service-oriented and helpful, balances ego with duty
- Organized and reliable, good at managing others

- Perfectionist tendencies, can be anxious about getting things wrong

### Nakshatra Refinements

- Combines analytical mind with warmth and generosity
#### Hasta (10-23.33°)

- Witty humor, good at mimicry, adaptable
#### Chitra (23.33-30°)
- More artistic and visually oriented, drawn to beauty and design
- Can be vain or overly focused on appearances, creative talent

- Confident and sometimes flashy, stands out more than typical Libra
#### Swati (6.67-20°)
- Independent, flexible, adaptable like wind
- Business-minded, good at trade and negotiation, values freedom
#### Vishakha (20-30°)
- More intense and goal-oriented, driven by ambition
- Single-minded pursuit of objectives, can be obsessive

#### Anuradha (3.33-16.67°)

- Ability to organize and lead groups, diplomatic Scorpio
#### Jyeshta (16.67-30°)
- "The Elder" — protective, authoritative, carries heavy responsibility

## Capricorn Rising (Makara Lagna)

### Physical Appearance
- Thin or bony build, especially when young — fills out with age
- Angular face, prominent bones (cheekbones, jawline)
- Serious expression, looks older than age when young, younger when old
- Measured, careful movements, nothing wasted
- Dark hair/complexion tendency, lean physique

### Personality & First Impression
- Serious, practical, focused on achievement and status
- Ambitious but patient — willing to climb slowly
- Responsible and dutiful, may carry burdens beyond their years
- Reserved initially, warms up slowly, dry humor
- Respects hierarchy, tradition, and institutions
- Can be cold, calculating, or overly focused on material success

### Nakshatra Refinements
#### Uttara Ashadha (0-10°)
- More idealistic, universal values, principled leadership
- Stands alone if needed, integrity over popularity
#### Shravana (10-23.33°)
- Excellent listener, skilled at learning through observation
- Connected to knowledge transmission, education, media
#### Dhanishta (23.33-30°)
- More musical/rhythmic, wealth-oriented, social confidence
- Group leadership ability, attracted to prestigious associations
