# Dasha Signatures for Rectification Questioning

Use these profiles to generate chart-specific questions about each Mahadasha.
Combine: Core Themes + Dignity Modifier + House Modifier to create the question options.

---

## Sun Mahadasha (6 years)

**Core Themes**: Authority, father, government, self-confidence, leadership, health/vitality, recognition, ego

**By Dignity**:
- **Exalted (Aries)**: Period of commanding authority, recognition without seeking it, confident leadership, father figure empowering
- **Own Sign (Leo)**: Natural self-expression, creative confidence, leadership roles, generous authority
- **Debilitated (Libra)**: Self-doubt masked by performance, authority challenged, father issues, identity crisis in relationships

**By House** (1-line each):
- 1st: Self-transformation through authority, health focus, personal power emerges
- 2nd: Wealth through government/authority, powerful speech, family pride
- 3rd: Courage amplified, authoritative communication, sibling dynamics with father
- 4th: Property/vehicle gains, mother-father dynamic central, inner confidence
- 5th: Creative brilliance, children significant, speculative gains, romance with authority figures
- 6th: Victory over enemies, health challenges then recovery, service to authority
- 7th: Partnerships with powerful people, marriage dynamics, public-facing authority
- 8th: Transformation through power, hidden authority, father's health, inheritance
- 9th: Fortune through father/guru, higher education, pilgrimage, dharmic recognition
- 10th: Peak career authority, government roles, public status, professional recognition
- 11th: Gains through authority, powerful network, fulfilled ambitions, elder sibling connects to power
- 12th: Authority exercised in isolation/foreign lands, government expenses, spiritual authority

**Event Signatures**: Promotion/authority role, father-related event, government interaction, health turning point
