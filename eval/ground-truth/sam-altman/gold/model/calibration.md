# Calibration: back-test of the sealed blind reading against the life (to 2022-06-30)

The blind reading was written from the chart alone and sealed before the life was seen. Here each of its period predictions is set against what actually happened, antardasha by antardasha, from birth to the sub-period running at the cut-off (Rahu-Mars, 11 days old). Every miss carries a reason, and every reason is labelled **strong** (visible beforehand from classical rules the blind method already used) or **weak** (a post-hoc stretch). Lessons become person-specific rules (rules.json) that carry forward.

## Scorecard

- Domain predictions: 69. Key-pratyantar checks: 18.
- Verdicts (domain predictions and pratyantar checks together): data-gap 35, partial 21, hit 18, stronger-influence 7, alternate-reading 5, outside-chart 1.
- 'Notably quiet' calls: 53, of which 51 were quiet-correct.
- Significant events (magnitude 3 or more) that no prediction covered: 13, all explained.
- Adult life is where the test has teeth. In the completed Rahu-MD sub-periods (Rahu-Rahu to Rahu-Moon, age 20.2-37.2) there are 28 domain predictions: 9 hit, 8 partial, 4 stronger-influence, 3 alternate-reading, 1 outside-chart and only 3 data-gap. The major misses are explained mostly by two rules: Mercury, Venus and Jupiter periods are career periods (rule-01), and Saturn means a restructure upward (rule-02).
- Childhood (Sun and Moon MD, 0-13.2) has 20 predictions: 16 data-gap and 4 partial. Childhood is mostly data-gap. That is a limit of the record, not a verdict on the chart (rule-10).

## Biggest lessons

1. **The D10 10th cluster was underweighted.** The blind called Rahu-Venus a 'consolidation' period and gave Rahu-Mercury a career strength of 3. Those two sub-periods held the presidency of Y Combinator, the co-founding of OpenAI, and the OpenAI CEO role plus the $1B Microsoft deal (rule-01).
2. **Saturn restructures upward.** Rahu-Saturn was a textbook setback (Facebook Places five days in, then a sale at a loss to investors), but it also opened the patron track that led to the YC presidency. The same pattern repeated in the Saturn pratyantar of 2019 (rule-02).
3. **Patrons are the channel.** Every rise ran through a sponsor (rule-04), and the conflicts were fights with sponsors and co-founders rather than lawsuits.
4. **Ashtama Shani hit the father.** The blind's 'health under ashtama Shani' became the father's death in May 2018 (Saturn transiting the 9th) (rule-07).
5. **Partnership timing came from transits, not Venus or Ketu sub-periods.** The first partnership ended under Saturn in Libra, and no marriage is recorded (rule-03, rule-06).
6. **Pratyantar timing worked for malefics.** The Saturn pratyantar landed on the 2008 crisis sale exploration. The Mars pratyantar (Dec 2017 to Feb 20 2018) closed on the day Musk left the OpenAI board (rule-12).
