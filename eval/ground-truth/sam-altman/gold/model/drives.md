## What blocks him

- **Distribution and competitive giants.** His first company lost to a platform incumbent (ev-033) and plateaued (ev-032).
- **Capital dependence.** His ventures need other people's money at scale. The lab ran short after a founding donor stopped funding (ev-059) and needed a $1B strategic partner (ev-068). The first startup needed a sale in a crisis (ev-028).
- **Trust and control frictions with partners and patrons.** The co-founder control fight (ev-058) and the contested framing of his exit from the accelerator (ev-064).
- **Image risk.** Growth-hack backlash (ev-025), a 'survivalist' profile (ev-055, ev-056), privacy criticism of the crypto launch (ev-076), and a sibling's public accusations (ev-078).
- **Overextension.** Many parallel roles (ev-034, ev-053, ev-045, ev-062) and overwork severe enough to cause scurvy at 20 (ev-017).
- **An inner life kept private.** His mother says he 'keeps an awful lot tied up inside' (ev-056). He worked out his identity alone before going public (ev-008).
