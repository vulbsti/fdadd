# Blind reading: method

## Inputs and conventions
- I read only chart.json, periods-by-age.json and transits-by-age.json. I made no attempt to identify the native.
- Conventions: Lahiri sidereal, whole-sign houses, true node, Vimshottari. Birth time was not rectified.
- Ages are taken from periods-by-age.json. There are 39 antardashas between birth and 50, from Sun-Jupiter to Jupiter-Moon (49.5-50.9).
- The transit data ends at about age 45. Saturn after 45 was extrapolated at about 2.5 years per sign: Taurus to about 47.5, then Gemini, so sade sati ends around 49-50.

## Rules applied
- Planets act through their lordship first, then their placement, then dignity and nakshatra. For Aries lagna: Mars rules the 1st and 8th, Venus the 2nd and 7th, Mercury the 3rd and 6th, Moon the 4th, Sun the 5th, Jupiter the 9th and 12th, Saturn the 10th and 11th.
- I checked neecha bhanga myself.
  - Mercury: cancelled (conjunct exalted Venus; its dispositor Jupiter is in a kendra).
  - Jupiter: also cancelled, which the software yoga list misses. Saturn, the lord of its debilitation sign, is in a kendra from the Moon. Mars, which exalts in Capricorn, is conjunct the Moon. Jupiter is exalted in D9. I treat this as a real NBRY that delivers in Jupiter MD, after early struggle.
- I re-checked the listed yogas. I accept Amala, Harsha VRY, the 4-1 raja yoga, Chandra-Mangala (orb 0.7 deg), Daridra (but softened by an 11th SAV of 34) and the Mercury NBRY.
  - Grahan is weak (16 deg orb).
  - Kemadruma is mostly cancelled.
  - Gaja Kesari and Malavya are not formed.
  - I added a raja yoga: the 5th lord Sun exalted in the lagna.
- Each antardasha is judged in three parts:
  - the AD lord's houses from the lagna, and its relation to the MD lord (natural and compound);
  - the AD lord's natal dignity;
  - what slow transits do to the lagna, the Moon and the AD lord's natal position.

## Weighing dasha and transit
- The dasha decides what can happen; transits decide when and how hard. I weighted them roughly 65/35.
- A transit was allowed to flip the direction of a domain only when it was a major Saturn event:
  - sade sati, at about 13-20 and 42-50;
  - ashtama Shani, at about 2.7-5.7 and 31.8-34.8;
  - Saturn return on natal Saturn, at about 29.5-31.8.
- Jupiter transits over the lagna, the Moon, the 9th, the 10th or natal Jupiter were used as amplifiers. Peaks:
  - 22.6-25: Jupiter through the 9th and over natal Jupiter;
  - 34.5-37: Jupiter and Saturn both through the 9th/10th;
  - 38-40: Jupiter over the lagna and the Moon, with Saturn in the 11th.
- Rahu transits over the lagna or Moon were read as turbulence: Rahu on the Moon at 35.4-36.9, Rahu on the lagna at 36.9-38.6.

## Dominant factors (in order)
1. The career complex. Jupiter is in the 10th with neecha bhanga; it is exalted in D9 and in its own sign in the D10 10th, alongside exalted Venus and Mercury. The 10th SAV is 36. This points to high career attainment.
2. Exalted Sun with Rahu in the lagna. This gives ambition, leadership, unconventional and foreign paths, and a polarising image. Rahu MD (20-38) is the formative adult period.
3. Saturn, the 10th/11th lord, retrograde in the 8th. Gains come through risk and other people's money, with real crises. Every Saturn AD and Saturn transit window is a pressure point.
4. Exalted Moon conjunct Mars in the 2nd. Enterprise-driven wealth, with a sharp tongue.
5. Exalted Venus as 7th lord in the 12th, with Ketu in the 7th. A foreign or unusual spouse, and partnership instability.

## Where the chart is ambiguous
- The timing of the peak.
  - The Rahu MD (early meteor) and Jupiter MD (NBRY delivery) readings compete.
  - My prior is a twin peak: 22.9-25.3, then 34.8-40.3, with a setback at 25-31 and a crisis at 42-47.
- The strength of the whole chart. The yoga picture is strong, but no planet meets its shadbala minimum. That could mean a notable-but-not-exceptional life rather than an outstanding one.
- Marriage timing. It could fall in Rahu-Mercury (28-31) or Rahu-Venus (31.8-34.8); I favour Rahu-Venus. A first partnership may end in Rahu-Ketu (30.7-31.8). The D9 supports the spouse while the rasi 7th afflicts the partnership, so the two signals conflict.
- Mercury. It is debilitated with neecha bhanga, gives Harsha VRY, and is debilitated again in D10. It could be read as weak learning in conventional settings or as winning through adversity. I gave both as candidates and favour the VRY reading for conflict.
- Childhood (0-13). The predictions are low-confidence. They rest mostly on family proxies, and exact events cannot be checked from dasha logic alone.
- Saturn after 45 is extrapolated, so the exact end of the sade sati is uncertain by about one year.
