## What I’d protect

Don’t wait for work to become calm before investing in these relationships. Keep a dependable rhythm with Oliver, and set specific time for your mom and brothers rather than relying on good intentions. With Annie, I wouldn’t use astrology to promise reconciliation; the more useful question is what supportive contact and boundaries are possible without forcing an outcome. And with your dad, you’ve said yourself that working was how you handled his death. Making room for stories about him—or for what you wish you’d asked—may be more important than trying to solve the grief by staying busy.
